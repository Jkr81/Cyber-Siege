01 — Design Intent

Mission Directive

⚡

Constant Forward Motion

Players are propelled through a hostile asteroid field under relentless pressure — no stopping, no retreating.

🌐

360° Spatial Awareness

The fully immersive VR environment demands constant awareness in every direction. Threats emerge from all angles.

📈

Escalating Tension

Scaling difficulty ensures the pressure never lets up — reaction time and positioning become increasingly critical.

02 — Core Systems

Core Mechanics

🚀 Movement System
  • Continuous forward movement through a curved asteroid tunnel
  • Player can shift position within the tunnel while guided along a path
  • Speed gradually increases to intensify gameplay over time
👾 Enemy System
  • Enemies spawn dynamically relative to the player's position
  • Multiple enemy types — standard variants and higher-threat units
  • Enemies track and attack the player within a configurable detection range
🔫 Combat System
  • Projectile-based enemy attack patterns
  • Player takes damage on impact with reliable hit detection
  • Damage scales as the player progresses deeper into the tunnel
📊 Progression & Difficulty
  • Higher enemy damage output over time
  • Faster movement pacing as depth increases
  • More consistent enemy presence with no lull periods
  • Score system with higher-value targets rewarding precision
🌌 Environment System
  • Procedurally generated asteroid tunnel
  • Adjustable width, curvature, and asteroid density
  • Moving asteroids to create dynamic environmental motion
03 — Problem Solving

Challenges & Solutions

Enemy Spawning & Pacing

Problem

Enemies appeared behind or below the player, with dead periods of zero enemy presence breaking the tension.

Solution

Refined spawn logic to ensure enemies always appear in front of or near the player, maintaining consistent pressure throughout.

Projectile Hit Detection

Problem

Bullets were unreliable — missing the player or colliding with unintended objects in the environment.

Solution

Reworked collision handling using proper collider hierarchy and GetComponentInParent() for consistent damage registration.

Enemy Aiming Behavior

Problem

Enemies fired inaccurately, shooting in incorrect directions and failing to properly track the player.

Solution

Implemented directional targeting toward the player with positional offsets and debug visualization for fine-tuning.

Difficulty Balancing

Problem

Score inflated too rapidly and damage scaling felt inconsistent, undermining meaningful progression.

Solution

Adjusted scaling systems to create a smoother difficulty curve and produce more satisfying moment-to-moment progression.

Tunnel & Environment

Problem

Tunnel felt too wide or empty, asteroids stopped unexpectedly, and the player could drift off the intended path.

Solution

Refined tunnel constraints, spawn distances, and movement boundaries to keep gameplay in a focused, intense corridor.

Visual Feedback & Game Feel

Problem

Missing impact effects and delayed or unclear enemy death animations reduced immersion and combat clarity.

Solution

Improved visual feedback timing to ensure effects trigger correctly on collisions and destruction events.
